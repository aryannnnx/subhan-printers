Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our printing services.

What printing services do you offer?

We offer a wide range of services including wedding cards, box packaging, flex banners, brochures, stickers, business cards, and custom printing solutions.

How long does it take to complete an order?

Standard orders are completed within 3-5 business days. Rush orders can be accommodated for an additional fee. Complex projects may take 7-10 days.

What file formats do you accept for designs?

We accept AI, PSD, PDF, CDR, EPS, and high-resolution JPG/PNG files (minimum 300 DPI). For best results, please provide vector files.

Do you offer design services?

Yes! Our in-house design team can create custom designs for you. Design charges are separate from printing costs and vary based on complexity.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Minimum quantities vary by product. Business cards start at 100 pieces, wedding cards at 50 pieces, and banners can be ordered as single pieces.

Do you deliver outside Lahore?

Yes, we deliver all across Pakistan via trusted courier services. Delivery charges and times vary by location.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash on delivery, bank transfer (HBL, Meezan, JazzCash, EasyPaisa), and online card payments through our secure checkout.

What if I'm not satisfied with the print quality?

We stand behind our quality. If you receive a defective print, contact us within 48 hours with photos and we'll reprint or refund your order.

Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order?

Yes, we provide physical or digital proofs before production. For bulk orders, we can create a sample print for your approval.

How can I contact customer support?

You can reach us via email at support@subhanprinters.com, call +92 300 1234567, WhatsApp us, or visit our shop in Gawalmandi, Lahore.
